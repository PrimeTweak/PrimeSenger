# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Privacy: read anonymously (always, with a manual eye or until you reply), hide the typing indicator, watch stories anonymously, block screenshot alerts
- Chats: hide the quick reaction, keep the keyboard closed, confirm before calling, upload in HD, block View once, and a mute bell that silences a chat on this phone only
- Chat list: hide the stories tray, People you may know and friend suggestions
- Stories: hide the reply bar and start stories with sound
- Media: unlock grayed-out actions, a save button for story, disappearing and profile pictures, reveal covered photos, replay View once photos, and loop, sound and speed for videos
- Meta AI: hide it in search, the chat list button and the media menu
- Tab bar: Liquid Glass and hidden tabs, any of the five
- Tools: Backup & reset to export, import and reset settings and clear the cache, a floating button that opens the settings, and FLEX explorer
- Compatibility report in debug builds
